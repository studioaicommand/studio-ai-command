SET UP A CLIENT'S OWN COMMAND (white-label)

Every white-label client gets their OWN separate Command. Never rebrand your own Command for a client
(your own Command now refuses to). Their copy has its own database, keys, web address, brand, AI name,
charter and company brief. None of your data, prices or offers are in it, and none of theirs are in yours.

What it makes, in about 5 minutes:
  - Their own database in Sydney (Supabase), 22 tables, all locked down
  - Their own Command at  <name>.studioflowandsystems.app  with its own keys
  - Their brand, industry template (charter, daily shifts) and features
  - Command reads their website so inbox replies know their services and FAQs
  - A Face ID sign-in link for the owner (and any staff you add)
  - Their plan (Core or Growth) and a short brief of what their business does, so their AI team
    works on THEIR services and prices, never Studio Flow's
  - It copies the exact version your own Command is running right now (straight from Cloudflare),
    so you no longer need to put a file in "1 Deploy Now" first

Before the first one (once only):
  1. Supabase: supabase.com > your avatar > Account > Access Tokens > Generate.
     Name it "Client setup". Each client database costs about US$10/month on the Pro plan.
  2. Cloudflare: dash.cloudflare.com > My Profile > API Tokens > Create Token > Custom token. Permissions:
       Account  > Workers Scripts > Edit
       Zone     > Workers Routes  > Edit
       Zone     > DNS             > Edit
       Zone     > Zone            > Read
     Zone Resources: Include > Specific zone > studioflowandsystems.app
  Keep both tokens in Apple Passwords. You paste them into the setup window each time (they are never saved).

Each new client:
  1. Deploy your own Command first if you have an update waiting (the client gets what you run).
  2. Double-click "New client Command.command". If macOS blocks it: right-click > Open > Open.
     (Or in Terminal: zsh ~/Desktop/"Studio AI Intelligence/Client setup kit/New client Command.command")
  3. Answer the questions. Have ready: their business name, owner name, website, industry, and
     their plan (core or growth), one or two sentences on what they do (services, prices, who they help),
     and optionally their GHL sub-account token + location ID, Telegram bot token and Stripe read key.
  4. Send the owner the sign-in link it prints (privately). When they first sign in, "Get set up" opens:
     11 steps, each checked live (brand, what they do, Face ID, team, Telegram, GoHighLevel, Stripe,
     phone, website chat, first goal, try it). They paste their own GoHighLevel, Stripe (read-only) and
     Telegram keys there: each key is checked first, then stored sealed in their copy. You don't need
     their keys at setup any more. Book a 30-minute onboarding call to help with anything left.
     Their progress shows on their card in Clients > Client Commands, and you get a Telegram heads-up
     if they're stuck for 2 days (they get a gentle nudge too).
     They open the link in Safari, set up Face ID, then Share > Add to Home Screen.
  5. The private details are saved in "Client setups". Put them in Apple Passwords, then delete the file.

Later changes for that client: open their Command > Settings > Brand & features.

See all clients in your Studio console:
  Your Command > Clients > Client Commands. Paste each client's connect code (it's printed at the end of
  setup and saved in their file in "Client setups"). You'll see each client's health, version and AI cost.
  You can't see their customers or messages. For that, tap "Ask for support access": their owner gets a
  Telegram message and approves it in their Settings > Security (24 hours, logged, and you can't send
  messages, take payments, see money or delete anything).

Update every client to the newest version:
  1. Deploy your own Command first. The update copies whatever version your own Command is running.
  2. Double-click "Update all clients.command" and paste your Cloudflare token.
  3. It updates your test client (any client with "test" in its web name) first, checks it, then asks
     before doing the rest. Every client keeps its own keys. If a check fails, that client is put back
     on its old version straight away and nobody else is touched. Backups go in "Client setups/backups".

Give a client their OWN web address (e.g. command.harbourphysio.com.au):
  Once only: Cloudflare > studioflowandsystems.app > SSL/TLS > Custom Hostnames > Enable (free for the first
  100 clients). Your Cloudflare token also needs "SSL and Certificates: Edit" (add it to the same custom token).
  1. Double-click "Connect client domain.command". Give it their web name and their address.
     It finds their connect code in "Client setups" (or paste it from Apple Passwords).
  2. It prints one CNAME record (and a TXT record). Send those to whoever manages their domain.
  3. Run it again after they add them: it says "Live" when the address is secure (usually 5 to 30 minutes).
  4. Their owner opens Command > Settings > Brand & features > "Set up Face ID on your new address", and
     "Links for the team" makes a link for each staff member. The old address keeps working.
  Their domain goes ONLY to their own Command. Running it again is safe.

Their colours across the whole app:
  Their Command > Settings > Brand & features: main colour, accent colour, and Dark or Light background.
  Backgrounds, panels, borders, buttons and the AI orb all take on their colours. Text stays readable
  whatever colour they pick.
