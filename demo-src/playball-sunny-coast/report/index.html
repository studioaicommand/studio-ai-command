Where enquiries slip through

HIGH

1. The "contact" links loop back to the same page

On the Sunny Coast page, "Contact your local team", "Check current local information" and "Contact the local team" all link back to the page you're already on. A parent ready to act has nowhere to go.

Fix: every button leads to booking, a party quote or a direct message to Luke.

HIGH

2. No local timetable, venues or prices

The page says "contact the team for the latest availability". Parents comparing activities on a Sunday night move on to whoever shows times and a "book" button.

Fix: live classes, venues and spots left, bookable from a phone.

HIGH

3. Party and camp enquiries wait for a reply

Parties and camps are time-sensitive. The first business to send a quote usually wins the booking.

Fix: an instant party-quote form and an AI receptionist that answers 24/7 and alerts Luke.

MEDIUM

4. Centre and school leads have no clear path

Early learning centre programs can be the steadiest income, but directors just get a generic form.

Fix: a "request a centre proposal" path with automatic follow-up.

MEDIUM

5. Hard to find locally

We couldn't find a Google Business Profile for "Playball Sunny Coast", and the local testimonials section is empty. Parents searching "kids sport Sunshine Coast" see competitors' reviews instead.

Fix: a Google Business Profile, plus automatic review requests after each term and party.

MEDIUM

6. The FAQ sends parents somewhere else

Answers like "Ask your local partner" appear on the local partner's own page, which reads as uncertainty.

Fix: local answers, by Luke, in Luke's voice.

What the fixes are worth

Each new family on weekly classes pays term after term, often for years and with siblings. One extra party a fortnight, or one more early learning centre program, adds steady income on top. On a call, we'll put Luke's real numbers into this so it's accurate, not a guess.
