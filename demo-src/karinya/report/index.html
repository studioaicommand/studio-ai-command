What's already brilliant

  • Rated Exceeding the National Quality Standard, which most Cooroy centres don't reach.
  • A 5.0 Google rating and heartfelt parent testimonials.
  • A genuinely special story: Montessori, Free Kindy, animals, garden, bush kindy, languages, music and sport.

The opportunity isn't the centre. It's that the website doesn't show any of this off or turn interest into tours.

Where families slip through

HIGH

1. No "Book a tour" path

Every enrolment starts with a tour ("after a tour of the centre a no-obligation quote can be emailed"), but the homepage has no tour button or booking. The contact page is a plain form with a fax number.

Fix: a "Book a tour" button everywhere and a tour booking form that confirms by text.

HIGH

2. No waitlist for full rooms

Toddle shows some age groups currently have no vacancies. Families who find a room full usually move on to the next centre instead of waiting.

Fix: a waitlist form (child's age + start date) and automatic updates when a place opens.

HIGH

3. Hard to find in Google

When we searched "montessori kindy Noosa Cooroy Sunshine Coast", Karinya's own website wasn't on page one. Directory sites were, and families who enquire there aren't on Karinya's own list.

Fix: a modern, fast site with local search terms (Montessori, kindy, Free Kindy, Cooroy, Noosa) and an updated Google Business Profile.

MEDIUM

4. Different prices in different places

The website lists $96–$140 a day (from July 2026). Toddle still shows $115 a session. Mixed numbers create doubt before a parent ever calls.

Fix: one clear fees section, with the Free Kindy and subsidy story explained simply.

MEDIUM

5. No answers after hours

Parents research at night and on weekends. Questions about fees, Free Kindy or vacancies wait until the next business day, by which time they've often enquired somewhere else.

Fix: an AI receptionist trained on Karinya's details that answers instantly, 24/7, and books tours.

MEDIUM

6. The website looks older than the centre

Page addresses like "copy-of-meet-the-karinya-team", a fax number, a spelling slip ("Reconcilliation") and an ageing template undersell an Exceeding-rated centre.

Fix: a calm, beautiful homepage that matches the experience inside the classroom.

What one extra family is worth

One child attending 3 long days a week:

≈ $20,000 a year
