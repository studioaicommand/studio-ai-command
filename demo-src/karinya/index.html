Our approach

It takes a village to raise a child.

Karinya supports each child's whole and natural development: independence, curiosity and kindness, in a peaceful environment where children are engaged in purposeful, meaningful work. You'll never feel alone as a parent here.

Trained Montessori educators

Caring, professional educators who genuinely love working with children.

A home visit before day one

Gentle orientation and settling, so your little one feels at home from the start.

Languages & culture

Second-language immersion, Gubbi Gubbi workshops and cultural celebrations through the year.

Music & sport every week

Music with Maggie Moo and sports sessions with Junior Sports Queensland.

Nature & animals

Where children care for living things.

Guinea pigs to cuddle (and take home on weekends), native rainbow fish fed every morning, tadpoles into frogs, caterpillars into butterflies, chickens hatching, a garden, bush kindy and a yearly hands-on Ocean Life visit.
