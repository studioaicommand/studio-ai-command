# Research kit

`replica-skill/` is Jake Schincariol's Replica skill pack (MIT, https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/replica-skill),
downloaded 15 Nov 2026 for reference. It is not part of Command and none of its scripts run in production.

How we use it: the method, not the cloning. Command's monthly "Evolve research" shift (Head of Systems) follows
replica-recon (map how competitors work from public sources) and replica-entrepreneur (rank what their real users
hate, verbatim and linked, never fabricated), then proposes ranked upgrades for Studio AI Command. Claude can also
load these skills when building, e.g. replica-test and replica-diff to test and score our own screens.

Rules carried over: public sources only, no logging into other people's accounts, no copying code, branding or
copy, no fake or invented reviews.
